PART 2 — FINANCE TO AI INTEGRATION

The existing AI Assistant currently does not see real Finance data.

Connect the existing Finance data to the AI context.

The AI must use real user data from the existing Finance store:

- income transactions;
- expense transactions;
- savings transactions;
- bills;
- bill statuses;
- bill due dates;
- monthly budgets;
- available money;
- savings totals;
- linked goals where available.

Do not duplicate data.
Do not create a separate AI Finance database.
Do not change the AI chat UI.

The AI must understand and answer questions such as:

- How much income do I have this month?
- How much have I spent this month?
- What are my biggest expense categories?
- Which bills are due soon?
- Which bills are overdue?
- How much money remains after known obligations?
- How much have I saved?
- How is my savings goal progressing?
- Did I spend more on food this month?
- What recurring payments do I have?
- What should I pay attention to before my next income?

The AI must use the expanded categories so it can answer naturally, for example:

- You spent 245 € on food this month.
- Your mobile, internet and TV bills total 89 €.
- Salary makes up most of your income.
- You have two subscription payments due this week.

Important:

- AI must never invent missing Finance data.
- If no Finance data exists, say so honestly.
- AI must distinguish between planned and actual amounts.
- AI must distinguish between upcoming, paid and overdue bills.
- AI must use the user’s selected language.
- AI must not make financial decisions for the user.
- AI may explain, summarize, compare and suggest.
- AI must not silently edit Finance data.

Testing requirements:

7. Confirm that the AI answers using the real saved data.
8. Refresh the page and confirm categories and Finance data persist.
